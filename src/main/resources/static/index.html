          How LedgerGuard keeps money correct
          • Double-entry ledger. Every transfer writes a debit and a matching credit. Balances are the sum of those entries, never a number someone can edit.
          • Safe retries. Each payment carries a unique key. Sending the same request again returns the original result instead of charging twice. Try "Retry this exact request" after a transfer.
          • No overdrafts. Accounts are locked while a transfer runs, so two payments can't spend the same money at the same time.
          • Fraud review. Each payment to another person is scored by rules (large amount, first payment to someone new, many payments in minutes, emptying the account). One signal alone is normal; two or more hold the payment for a fraud analyst, and no money moves until they approve.
